Who We Are

Carbon Neutral Advisory Services (CNAS) is an environmental consulting firm registered with the Environmental Management Agency (EMA) of Zimbabwe.

What We Do

We provide expert environmental consulting, industrial training, and innovative educational solutions to clients across Zimbabwe, Botswana, and South Africa.

Our Mission

To guide enterprises toward verifiable Net-Zero pathways and climate resilience while empowering communities through education.

Our Team

We are a team of experienced environmental professionals passionate about sustainability and education.

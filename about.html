About Carbon Neutral Advisory Services

Who We Are

Carbon Neutral Advisory Services (CNAS) is an environmental consulting firm serving clients across Zimbabwe, Botswana and South Africa.

What We Do

We provide environmental consulting, industrial training, academic research support and applied technology solutions to clients across Zimbabwe, Botswana and South Africa. Our work spans regulatory assessments, safety and quality systems, capacity building, research support, and monitoring and evaluation.

Our Mission

To guide enterprises toward verifiable net-zero pathways and climate resilience, while empowering communities through education and skills transfer.

Our Approach

  • Regulatory compliance first, so that every deliverable stands up to scrutiny.
  • Baseline evidence and measurable indicators rather than generic recommendations.
  • Skills transfer, so that clients can sustain systems after our engagement ends.
